ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY & PERFORMANCE REPORT FY2025 — ABC INDUSTRIES LTD.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & KEY CLAIMS:
During Fiscal Year 2025 (FY25), ABC Industries Ltd. continued its commitment to operational excellence and environmental stewardship across all manufacturing facilities and corporate offices.

EMISSIONS PERFORMANCE:
- Achieved a 5.16% reduction in total Scope 1 & Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions across all operating regions in FY25 compared to FY24 baseline performance.
- Scope 1 direct emissions from company fleet and on-site fuel combustion decreased by 4.88% year-over-year.
- Scope 2 indirect emissions from purchased electricity fell by 5.35% compared to FY24.
- In FY24, aggregate emissions measured 10,228.05 metric tons CO2e. Through targeted energy efficiency retrofits and renewable energy purchases, total emissions for FY25 dropped to 9,700.00 metric tons CO2e.

ENERGY PERFORMANCE:
- Renewable energy now constitutes 48.33% of total energy consumption in FY25, up from 43.33% in FY24.
- Total energy consumption remained stable at 120,000 MWh, demonstrating our decoupling of growth from energy usage.

WATER STEWARDSHIP:
- Facility water consumption reduced by 2.68% in FY25 compared to FY24.
- Water recycling rate improved to 26.21% of total facility water usage.

WASTE REDUCTION:
- Solid waste generation decreased by 4.82% year-over-year, from 830 tons in FY24 to 790 tons in FY25.

We remain dedicated to transparent, verified reporting of all key performance indicators under BRSR disclosure requirements.
