ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT FY2025 — GREENTECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
GreenTech Solutions is proud to report unprecedented sustainability achievements across all divisions in FY25.

EMISSIONS OVERVIEW:
- We achieved a remarkable 35% reduction in total Scope 1 & Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions in FY25 compared to FY24.
- Our Scope 1 emissions decreased from 8,500 MT CO2e in FY24 to 8,200 MT CO2e in FY25.
- Scope 2 emissions were reduced from 12,000 MT CO2e to 11,500 MT CO2e over the same period.

ENERGY HIGHLIGHTS:
- Renewable energy comprises 65% of our total energy portfolio.
- Total energy consumption was reduced by 12% year-over-year.

WATER MANAGEMENT:
- Water consumption fell by 8% compared to the previous fiscal year, a testament to our closed-loop cooling systems.

These results position GreenTech Solutions as a leader in sustainable manufacturing across the region.
